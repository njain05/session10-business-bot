What we make

Winter wear and hosiery, made in our own knitting unit.

Sweaters

Men's and women's: V-neck, round neck and cardigans. Acrylic and wool-blend.

Jackets

Quilted jackets and fleece jackets.

Thermal wear

Thermal tops and bottoms for men, women and children.

Kids' winter wear

Sweaters, caps and mufflers for ages 2 to 12.

Accessories

Woollen caps, mufflers, gloves and shawls.

Adult sizes S to XXL. Colours from our standard shade card of 24 colours.

How buying works

We sell wholesale only, to shops and wholesalers. We do not sell single pieces to the public.

Minimum order
100 pieces per design, mixed across sizes and colours.
Prices
Depend on design, fabric and quantity. Our team sends a price list and quote on WhatsApp within one working day.
Delivery time
Ready stock dispatches in 3 to 5 working days. Made-to-order designs take 15 to 25 days.
Shipping
By transport company to anywhere in India. Freight is paid by the buyer.
Payment
50% advance, balance before dispatch. Bank transfer or UPI. No credit for new buyers.
Private label
Your own brand tag, from 500 pieces per design.
Samples
Up to 5 sample pieces by courier. The buyer pays the sample and courier cost.

Busy season is August to December. Order early for October delivery.
